Route Optimization Using Quantum Computing—a cutting-edge approach to solving one of the most complex and impactful problems in modern transportation and logistics.

As urbanization and global connectivity continue to grow, efficient route planning has become more crucial than ever. Traditional algorithms, while powerful, often struggle with large-scale, dynamic environments where multiple constraints like traffic congestion, road closures, and real-time conditions come into play. This is where quantum computing offers a revolutionary shift.
 Our project focuses on using Qiskit and other quantum tools to construct and evaluate quantum circuits for optimizing routes in real-world scenarios.
